# Statecraft Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Statecraft (published in Chinese as “Statecraft 治国”) respects your privacy. This policy applies to the iPhone game and related support contact.

## What we collect

We do not collect, sell, or share personal data. The game has no account, server, analytics SDK, or advertising SDK.

## Local data

Saves and game settings stay on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.

## Purchases

Apple processes the one-time unlock through StoreKit. We do not access or retain your payment information. You can use Restore Purchases in the game; Apple handles the information needed to process purchases and restores.

## Error-log export

You may choose to export an error log from Settings. It is not sent to us automatically; it leaves your device only if you choose a recipient in the iOS share sheet.

## Children

The game is not directed to children under 13.

For privacy questions, contact: brian_tiong@outlook.com
