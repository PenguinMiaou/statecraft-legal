# Statecraft Support

## How do I restore purchases?

Choose Restore Purchases from the title screen, Settings, or the end of the trial. The game asks Apple to restore verified purchases associated with your current Apple Account. If restoration fails, check your network and Apple Account status, then try again.

## Does the one-time unlock support Family Sharing?

Yes. The unlock has Apple Family Sharing enabled. Availability and eligibility remain subject to Apple’s Family Sharing rules and your family-group settings.

## How does the trial work?

The first term is free through the first completed election and government formation, normally quarter 16. If that election is lawfully postponed or must be retried, the trial continues until the term is actually complete. You can then make a one-time unlock to continue the same game through 32 quarters and use sandbox mode.

## Where are saves kept?

Saves stay on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. Deleting the app or changing devices may affect local saves; handle them according to your device and backup settings.

## How do I send feedback?

Send questions, suggestions, or steps to reproduce an issue to brian_tiong@outlook.com. If possible, include the game version, device model, and iOS version.

## How do I export an error log?

Open Settings, choose Export Error Log, then choose how to share it in the iOS share sheet. The log is never sent automatically; share it only if you choose to.
